AutoReel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

AutoReel is a personal, single-operator content creation tool. It generates short videos and publishes them to the operator's own social media accounts after manual approval. It is not offered as a service to the public and has no users other than its operator.

Information we collect

AutoReel does not collect, store, or process personal information from any third party. The only data it handles are:

  • OAuth access tokens for the operator's own social media accounts, stored locally on the operator's private server and used solely to publish content to those accounts;
  • Generated media files (video, audio, images) created by the tool itself.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data about viewers of published content.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not access other users' accounts, messages, or data on any platform.

Data retention and deletion

Access tokens are retained only while the tool is in use and can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's security settings. Generated media is stored on the operator's private server and deleted at the operator's discretion.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact the operator via the GitHub repository at github.com/jakebilsland-cloud/autoreel-legal.

AutoReel is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with TikTok, Google, Meta, or any other platform.
